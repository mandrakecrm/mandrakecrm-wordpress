== Installation ==

1. Upload the plugin folder to the `/wp-content/plugins/` directory
2. Activate the plugin through the 'Plugins' menu in WordPress
3. Go to WooCommerce > MandrakeCRM to configure the plugin
4. Create your account at [app.mandrakecrm.io/en/signup](https://app.mandrakecrm.io/en/signup) if you don't have one
5. Get your token at [Settings → Store](https://app.mandrakecrm.io/en/settings/?tab=store) and enter it in the plugin
6. Enable the features you want to use
7. Activate pre-configured automations in MandrakeCRM and start selling more

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Do I need a MandrakeCRM account? =

Yes. Visit [app.mandrakecrm.io/en/signup](https://app.mandrakecrm.io/en/signup) for a 7-day free trial — no credit card required.

= How much does MandrakeCRM cost? =

We charge per orders received, not per contacts. Your list grows at no extra cost. Recalculated every cycle based on the last 3 months. Slow season? You pay less. Find your monthly cost on our pricing page: [www.mandrakecrm.com/en/pricing](https://www.mandrakecrm.com/en/pricing/)

= Will I be charged after the 7-day trial? =

No. Enjoy all features for 7 days with no credit card. No hidden charges.

= How is it different from Klaviyo or Mailchimp? =

MandrakeCRM integrates CRM, automations, campaigns, and analytics in a single platform. Everything pre-configured. And charges per order, not per contact.

= How long does setup take? =

5-10 minutes. Install the plugin, paste your token, activate features. Your customers, orders, and products sync automatically.

= Do I need technical knowledge to use MandrakeCRM? =

No. Automations come pre-configured by experts. Just choose, activate, and go. If you can manage your WooCommerce store, you can use MandrakeCRM.

= Will my emails end up in spam? =

No. We send from your own domain (marketing@yourstore.com) following deliverability best practices. Your emails reach the inbox.

= Which WooCommerce emails are replaced? =

9 customer emails: New Account, Order Processing, Completed, On Hold, Refunded, Cancelled, Failed, Customer Note, and Password Reset. Admin emails stay with WooCommerce.

= Does it track guest checkout carts? =

Yes. Tracks both registered users and guest checkouts for abandoned cart recovery.

= Is it compatible with HPOS and Block Checkout? =

Yes. Fully compatible with WooCommerce High-Performance Order Storage and modern block-based checkout.

= Will this slow down my site? =

No. Async processing on cloud servers means zero performance impact on your store.

= What if I need help? =

Our support team is available via chat and email in English, Portuguese, and Spanish. We typically respond within 24 hours (usually much faster): support@mandrakecrm.com

== External Services ==

This plugin connects to MandrakeCRM external services for CRM, email marketing, and abandoned cart recovery functionality.

= MandrakeCRM API =

**Provider:** MandrakeCRM
**Endpoint:** https://mandrakecrm.supabase.co/functions/v1
**Terms of Service:** https://www.mandrakecrm.com/en/terms-of-service
**Privacy Policy:** https://www.mandrakecrm.com/en/privacy-policy

**Data transmitted:**
- License verification: token, plugin/WordPress/WooCommerce/PHP versions, site URL
- Transactional emails: customer email, order details, billing/shipping addresses
- Abandoned carts: cart contents, customer email, recovery URL

= MandrakeCRM CDN =

**URL:** https://cdn.mandrakecrm.io
**Purpose:** Load lead capture widgets
**Data transmitted:** None (static assets only)

= Important Notes =

- No data is transmitted until you configure the plugin with your token
- All transmission occurs over HTTPS
- You can disable specific features in settings
- By using this plugin, you agree to MandrakeCRM's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy
